What is Research

  • The pursuit of new knowledge through the process of discovery. Purdue University
  • Two grammars of the word: Open English Wordnet
    • Noun — a search for knowledge. Verb — to find out systematically.
  • The systematic, intentional process of asking questions, gathering evidence, interpreting data, and generating knowledge. ChatGPT

What each stem commits you to
