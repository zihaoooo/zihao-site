LAAR 61400 — Fall 2026

Final Deck — Due Dec 21

WF02 and WF06 no longer have their own submissions. Everything is due together on Dec 21, in your final deck, but each will still be graded separately.

What goes in the deck (20%)

One InDesign deck, exported as one PDF, in this order:

  1. Cover and contents
  2. WF01 AutoCAD Site Drafting — 3 pages: plan, section, and trace analysis
  3. WF04 Rhino Landform Modeling — 1 page
  4. WF05 Attractor Fields — 1 page
  5. WF06 Back to Basics: Harlem plaza sheets — 3 pages minimum
  6. Three best studio drawings, captioned — 3 pages
  7. 10 best sketchbook scans, white-balanced in Photoshop — as many pages as you need; several sketches can share a page
Graded separately inside the deck
  • WF06 Back to Basics — 20% — your hand sheets of the Harlem plaza, three at minimum. Scan or photograph them, then make light edits in Photoshop for white balance and contrast. A satellite overlay is no longer required.
  • Studio drawings — 10% — WF02 Hybrid Drawing is now a tutorial session with no assignment; its 10% goes to your three best studio drawings. Give each a caption describing its workflow as a chain of steps, for example: CAD linework – Illustrator – Photoshop – hand drawing – InDesign.
Submit

Bring your deck in progress to the desk review on Dec 3. Post the final PDF to Miro by Dec 21. See the weekly schedule.
