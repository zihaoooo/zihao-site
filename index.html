Zihao Zhang is an associate professor at the City College of New York Spitzer School of Architecture. He studies landscape as a product of technology and labor, asking what machines, tools, and unseen work of care produce the environments we call natural, and how that care is distributed among humans and nonhumans, including intelligent machines. He develops theory through making, using prototyping with sensors, actuators, and fabrication as a research method. His book Cybernetics and the Constructed Environment (Routledge, 2025) examines how cybernetic thinking reshaped the modern idea of nature. He is a co-founder of OSE (Office of Speculative Ecologies), a design practice working across climate adaptation, urban ecology, and social justice.

He holds a PhD in the Constructed Environment and an MLA from the University of Virginia, and a BEng in Landscape Architecture from Beijing Forestry University. He co-hosts Chubby Cat Happy Chat, a Mandarin podcast on design, philosophy, art, and technology.

张子豪现任纽约城市学院 Spitzer 建筑学院副教授。他的研究关注景观背后的技术与劳动，追问那些被视作自然的环境由何种机器、工具与不可见的照料维持，以及这些照料如何在人、其他生命与智能机器之间分配。他以制作展开理论研究，传感器、驱动器与数字建造既是媒介，也是方法。专著 Cybernetics and the Constructed Environment（Routledge, 2025）考察控制论如何重塑了现代的自然观念。他亦是 OSE 的联合创始人，设计实践围绕气候适应、城市生态与社会公正展开。

毕业于弗吉尼亚大学（建成环境博士、景观建筑硕士）及北京林业大学（风景园林本科）。是中文播客《猫肥家润》的主播之一，内容涵盖设计、哲学、艺术与技术。
