A Hole in the Park

2026 · Land ArtOSE

Collaborators
  • Savona Bailey-McClain — Executive Director & Chief Curator, West Harlem Art Fund / Harlem Sculpture Gardens
  • Shurui Zhang — OSE and McHarg Fellow
Part of
  • Harlem Lane Activation — Harlem Sculpture Gardens

The modern urban park bears the legacy of the Olmstedian picturesque tradition, which stems from landscape painting: the countryside was aestheticized by painting out the people whose labor and care made it. Entering a public park as a work of land art, this project proposes a pit and mound on the site left bare by the footings of a sculpture installed the year before. The land art suspends mowing on ground that is supposed to be lawn. Where the maintenance regime pauses, something else begins, and the lawn starts showing what else it could become. What grows back is weedy and unauthored, and one plant is poisonous enough for the Parks Department to ask that it be removed. Landscape as mischievous practice: it puts labor back into the picture, and asks whose care, under whose watch, decides what a park is allowed to become.
